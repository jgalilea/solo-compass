# Code of conduct

Solo Compass follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and kind, assume good faith, criticise ideas and not people, and remember that many people here play and write in a language that isn't their first. Harassment, insults or personal attacks of any kind aren't tolerated, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else in the project.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through the app's feedback form ([English](https://tally.so/r/M1NJQM), [español](https://tally.so/r/2jaJxb)): only the maintainer reads it, and reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments or block anyone who breaks these rules.
